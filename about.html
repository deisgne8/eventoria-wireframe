01 / About Eventoria

We know the stories
this country celebrates.

Eventoria is a UAE creative and production company for ceremonies, destinations and launches. We build the experience from the first sketch to the final show.

02 / CEO Message

A message
from our CEO.

At Eventoria, we believe every meaningful experience begins with a story worth telling. Our ambition is to bring that story to life with imagination, care and a deep understanding of the people and places it represents.

From the first conversation to the final cue, we bring creative thinking and practical expertise together. We listen closely, work collaboratively and care for the details, creating moments that connect people and stay with them long after the event.

Omar Al Noor

Chief Executive Officer

03 / Three beliefs

01

Place is not a backdrop.

Culture, identity and ambition are the source of the idea, not decoration applied at the end.

02

The idea must survive contact.

A good concept becomes stronger when it meets the venue, the audience, the cast, the schedule and the protocol.

03

One team carries the vision.

Creative and production stay connected, so nothing is lost between the story and the live moment.
