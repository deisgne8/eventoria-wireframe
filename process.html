01 / Process

Five steps.
One project team.

The right amount of structure keeps the creative moving, the practical details visible and the final show calm.

01

Listen

Brief, RFP and objectives translated into the real question. We establish the occasion, audience, place and measure of success.

02

Imagine

Story, concept and the creative direction behind it. A clear idea gives every later decision a reason.

03

Design

Experience, spatial and scenic development made tangible through sketches, references, plans and prototypes.

04

Produce

Build, cast, rehearse and prepare every practical detail, with the creative and the operational held together.

05

Deliver

Live show, operations and protocol carried through to the end by one team with one view of the moment.
